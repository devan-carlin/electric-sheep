# Multi-Step Math & Word Problems

Solve the following problems. Show your work step by step. Final answers should be boxed or clearly stated.

---

## Problem 1: Compound Interest with Withdrawals

An account starts with $10,000 at 6% annual interest, compounded monthly. At the end of month 6, $2,000 is withdrawn. At the end of month 12, another $2,000 is withdrawn. What is the balance at the end of month 18?

## Problem 2: Relative Speed

Train A leaves station X at 80 km/h heading east. Train B leaves station Y (200 km east of X) at 60 km/h heading west. Both depart at 9:00 AM. At what time and what distance from station X do they pass each other?

## Problem 3: Probability

A bag contains 5 red, 3 blue, and 2 green marbles. Two marbles are drawn without replacement. What is the probability that:
a) Both are red?
b) One is red and one is blue?
c) At least one is blue?

## Problem 4: Optimization

You have 100 meters of fencing to enclose a rectangular area against a straight wall (the wall serves as one side — no fencing needed there). What dimensions maximize the enclosed area, and what is that maximum area?

## Problem 5: Sequence

Find the 20th term of the sequence defined by:
- a(1) = 1
- a(2) = 1
- a(n) = a(n-1) + a(n-2) for n > 2

Then calculate the ratio a(20) / a(19) to 6 decimal places. What mathematical constant does this approach?
